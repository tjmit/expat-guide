---
<!-- Drafting note kept from source: Todd's cautionary story goes before this section. -->

1. Are you licensed to prepare US returns (EA, CPA, attorney) and do you handle Portuguese IRS too, or do you coordinate with someone who does? Who signs which return?
2. For my facts, should I use the FEIE, the foreign tax credit, or both across years, and what happens if I switch later?
3. Do I need Form 8938 and FBAR this year? Which of my accounts (including pensions, insurance and investment funds) count?
4. Are any of my foreign funds or ETFs PFICs (Form 8621)? What does that cost?
5. How does Portugal tax my US retirement accounts and Social Security, and what does the treaty say about each? Can you show me the article text?
6. Can I keep making 401(k)/403(b) or IRA contributions while abroad, and does it save me any tax?
7. Do I stay a resident of my US state for tax purposes? What should I do about my driver's licence, voter registration and bank accounts?
8. Am I a Portuguese tax resident, and from which date? What registration steps and deadlines apply?
9. Am I eligible for IFICI or any special regime? What is the deadline and what happens if I miss it?
10. What are all the deadlines (US April 15 / June 15 / October 15, FBAR, Portuguese April to June) and what happens on a late filing?
11. If I have past unfiled or incorrectly filed years, what options exist to fix them and what penalties could apply?
12. What documents should I keep and for how long?
13. Will I owe estimated or advance payments anywhere?
14. My employer says I owe nothing in Portugal under a treaty article for teachers. Is that a position I can rely on personally? Which conditions apply to me, who documents them, and what if the tax authority disagrees later?
15. What is your fee structure, and what is included if I get a letter from the IRS or the Portuguese tax authority?
