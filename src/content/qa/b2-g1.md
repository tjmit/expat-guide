Yes. IMT lists the United States among OECD countries whose licences stay valid after you become resident, if the licence is valid, was issued or last renewed less than 15 years ago, and you are under 60. Exchange is optional and needs no driving exam.

## Citations and legal explanation

- IMT conditions: issuing state signed a traffic convention (Geneva 1949 or Vienna 1968) or has a bilateral agreement; no more than 15 years since issue or last renewal; holder under 60; licence valid and not seized, suspended or revoked; holder at least the Portuguese minimum age for the category. The legal basis named is Decreto-Lei 46/2022.
- If you choose or need to exchange: medical certificate through the electronic platform; IMT says exchange is exempt from exams for the categories you exchange. Fee 30 euros with 10% off online. Documents: ID, residence communication or address proof, passport if the residence document lacks biometrics, the licence, a psychological assessment for categories C, D or E, and a certificate of authenticity from the issuing authority or consulate. You attend an IMT counter for biometrics.
- You cannot add categories or endorsements without exchanging.
