The status has no expiry. The card is renewed every 5 years.

## Citations and legal explanation

Art. 76: “não tem limite de validade”. The card is renewed every five years or when identity data change, and documents already in AIMA’s system need not be resubmitted. Renewal goes through the renewals portal.
