Yes, through the oral health cheques of the national programme (PNPSO) for children up to 18, pregnant women, people on the elderly solidarity supplement, people with HIV and people at increased oral-cancer risk, plus referral where clinically needed. Routine adult dental care is not listed as a cheque group.

## Citations and legal explanation

- Art. 3: the programme covers all SNS-registered users and targets the groups above, plus users needing prosthetic rehabilitation and users whose clinical needs require referral to dentistry or stomatology.
- Art. 16 values: children's first cheque 40 euros and later treatment cheques 45 euros; pregnant women, supplement beneficiaries and people with HIV 45 euros; oral-cancer project diagnostic 20 euros and biopsy 50 euros; reviewed at least every two years.
- Art. 33: the portaria revokes Portaria 430/2023, entered into force the day after publication (20 March 2026) and takes effect from 1 January 2027. Which rules apply until then: [UNVERIFIED].
