Not for new arrivals. It was revoked from January 1, 2024, and continues only for people already registered on that date plus a transitional group who became resident by 31 December 2024 and met stated proof conditions.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** AT leaflet (May 2024): the regime was revoked from 01/01/2024 and replaced by IFICI; it continues for those registered as NHR on 01/01/2024, running 10 years from the first year. Transitional group (leaflet, citing art. 236 nos. 3 to 5): resident by 31/12/2024, age 18+, not resident in the prior five years, and holding at least one listed proof, such as a work promise, contract or secondment agreement signed by 31/12/2023; a lease or purchase reservation by 10/10/2023; school enrolment of dependants before 10/10/2023; or a visa or residence permit (or a started procedure) by 31/12/2023. Three AT binding rulings quote n.º 3: one denies a person who became resident in 2024 without a qualifying 2023 step; one denies a person who had not become resident by 31/12/2024; one accepts a person with a work promise signed 19 Dec 2023 who applied by 31/03/2025. Requests after 31/03/2025 only run for the remaining period to the 10th year from 2024. Someone arriving now cannot get NHR.
