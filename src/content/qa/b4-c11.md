Yes, on an earlier bill. On 15 Dec 2025 the Court found four provisions unconstitutional. It did not rule against the other norms, including the 10-year period. The law that was published differs from that bill.

## Citations and legal explanation

Acórdão 1133/2025 (preventive review of Decreto da Assembleia da República n.º 17/XVII). The four provisions: an automatic bar for any conviction of 2 years or more (Art. 6(1)(f)); the rule that consolidation does not operate for “manifest fraud” (Art. 12-B(3)); assessing pending applications under the rules at the date of filing rather than at decision (Art. 7(3)-(4) of the decree); and cancelling registration for behaviour “rejecting adherence to the national community” (Art. 9(1)(a), second part; one dissent). The published Lei Orgânica 1/2026 differs: an effective sentence over 3 years, weighed case by case by the public prosecutor (Art. 6(14)); consolidation not operating where nationality was obtained “de forma fraudulenta”; and pending procedures stay under the old law (Art. 7(2)). Whether these changes fully resolve the Court’s objections was not analysed.
