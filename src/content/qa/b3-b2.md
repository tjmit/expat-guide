You need a foreign tax home plus either the bona fide residence test or the physical presence test. It covers pay for personal services only, not pensions or Social Security.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** Physical presence test: at least 330 full days in a foreign country or countries during any 12 consecutive months. Bona fide residence test: bona fide resident of a foreign country for an uninterrupted period that includes an entire tax year. The IRS "not foreign earned income" list includes pension or annuity payments, including Social Security benefits. Foreign earned income is defined as pay for personal services you performed; investment income is not addressed on the page I read. You do not have a foreign tax home if your abode remains in the US.
