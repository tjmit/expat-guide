$18. Request it online (fastest), by mailing a fingerprint card, or through an FBI-approved channeler for an extra fee.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** "$18 per person. You cannot pay extra for faster service." Payment: credit card using the FBI's payment form, or a money order or certified check to the "Treasury of the United States"; no cash, personal or business checks. Fingerprints go on a standard form FD-1164, taken "with ink or via live scan"; the FBI suggests a fingerprinting technician, possibly at a local police department. Processing: online requests "within 48 hours of visiting a United States Post Office location for electronic fingerprints"; mailed card "approximately 15 days (after we receive your mailed fingerprint card)"; mail requests "up to 50 days." Mail: FBI CJIS Division – Summary Request, 1000 Custer Hollow Road, Clarksburg, WV 26306. Channelers "may only process requests for a U.S. citizen or U.S. lawful permanent resident located in the U.S." Whether you can request or fingerprint from abroad: the page does not say. **[UNVERIFIED]**
