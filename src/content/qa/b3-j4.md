Online through the Portal das Finanças. For 2025 income the period was 1 April to 30 June 2026. I found no official dates yet for the next return.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** gov.pt: filing period 1 April to 30 June, online only. The AT newsletter (Jan 2026) says the 2025 return was to be filed "entre 1 de abril e 30 de junho de 2026". Tax due is payable by 31 August if assessed by 31 July (or 31 December if assessed by 30 November). Corrections have a 30-day window; complaints within 120 days. Check the Portal das Finanças for the next period.
