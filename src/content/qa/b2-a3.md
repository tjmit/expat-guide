Ask for renewal no later than 30 days before it expires. AIMA must decide within 60 days, and the renewal receipt has the same effect as your permit for 60 days, renewable.

## Citations and legal explanation

- Art. 78(1): renewal must be requested up to 30 days before expiry. Art. 78(2): renewal requires means of subsistence, housing and tax and Social Security compliance. Art. 78(7): the receipt has the effect of the permit for 60 days, renewable. Art. 82(6): decision within 60 days.
- gov.pt says renewal is requested online through the AIMA portal, that you can ask from 90 days before expiry, and that a permit expired for less than six months can still be renewed. The 90-day window is not in the law, so treat it as PROBABLY TRUE (official guidance, rule not found in the statute). The gov.pt page carries a banner that it may be out of date.
- IRN FAQ: IRN stopped handling renewal appointments on 1 August 2025. PROBABLY TRUE (official FAQ, not rechecked). Renewal fee: [UNVERIFIED].
