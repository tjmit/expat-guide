For Art. 88 employment permits, yes: tell AIMA, no new card is needed. Not confirmed for Art. 90 teaching permits.


**UNVERIFIED:** Art. 88(8) for Art. 90 permits

## Citations and legal explanation

Art. 88(8): the holder may change employer “mediante comunicação à AIMA, IP” without a new title. Art. 88(5) lets an employee permit holder take up self-employment by replacing the card. I did not verify that Art. 90 holders get the same rule.
