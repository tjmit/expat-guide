The VFS pages read do not list a NIF, but you can request one before arriving.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** The Portal das Finanças says registration "is mandatory for all persons - nationals or foreigners, residents or non-residents," and that non-residents can request a NIF without appointing a tax representative. Routes: in person at a Finanças office or Loja do Cidadão by appointment; the online route "must be submitted by a legal representative." Documents: passport or ID, valid entry visa if from a third country, proof of address abroad, certified translations of foreign-language documents. A tax representative or an electronic notification channel may be required "within 15 days" if a legal tax relationship arises (employment, property, vehicle); third-country self-employed people must appoint one. The NIF is issued with non-resident status and must be updated when you become a tax resident. Whether you can get it fully remotely without a representative: **[UNVERIFIED]**.
