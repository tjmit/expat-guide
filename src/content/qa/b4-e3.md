Spouse, minor or dependent children, adopted minors, unmarried adult children studying in Portugal, and dependent parents. Unmarried partners can qualify.

## Citations and legal explanation

Art. 99(1): spouse; minor or incapable children dependent on the couple or one spouse; adopted minors; unmarried adult children studying in Portugal; dependent ascendants in first degree of the resident or spouse; minor siblings under guardianship. Art. 99(6): reunification with a spouse or equivalent requires a marriage or union valid and recognised under Portuguese law, and both partners at least 18 at the date of the request. Art. 100: de facto partners and their unmarried minor children. Art. 99(4): reunification with a child of one spouse needs the other parent’s authorization or a custody decision.
