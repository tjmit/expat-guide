Request it online through the Segurança Social portal. A government notice says foreigners can now both request and obtain it online.

## Citations and legal explanation

- gov.pt NISS service page (3 Sept 2026): the request is online. Government news of 13 August 2025: it is now possible to request and obtain the NISS online, with biometric identity checks. Free of charge per ePortugal.
- Documents: earlier gov.pt text listed passport, residence permit or other civil ID and no visa alone. The current page (updated 19.10.2026 per the review) no longer lists documents, so [UNVERIFIED].
- ePortugal still describes collecting the NISS in person, likely outdated.
