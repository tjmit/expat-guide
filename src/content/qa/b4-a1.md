The first permit is valid for 2 years. Renewals are for successive 3-year periods.

## Citations and legal explanation

Art. 75(1) of Lei 23/2007 sets two years from issue, renewable for successive periods of three years, without prejudice to special rules. The card must also be replaced whenever the identification data on it changes (Art. 75(4)).
