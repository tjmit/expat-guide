It appears not. The statute, the profession list and the CAE list name only higher-education teaching and research.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** Alínea a) is "Docência no ensino superior e investigação científica". The only education profession in the highly qualified list (FAQ, Portaria annex) is "231 - Professor dos ensinos universitário e superior". The only education business activity is higher education (subclass 85420). The FCT, which certifies alínea a), describes only higher-education teaching and research. A university teacher on a service contract is covered (FAQ). Nothing in the texts mentions primary, secondary or international schools either way. Confirm with the tax authority before relying on it.
