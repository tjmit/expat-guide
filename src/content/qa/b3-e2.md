Yes. SSA pays US citizens abroad and direct deposit to a Portuguese account is listed. The US still taxes up to 85% of benefits, and Portugal is not on the list of countries exempt from US tax on benefits.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** SSA publication EN-05-10137: "If you are a United States citizen, you may continue to receive payments while outside the U.S." The countries where SSA generally cannot pay do not include Portugal; SSA's direct-deposit list includes Portugal (it names "some" countries). Tax: the SSA publication says up to 85% of benefits may be taxable regardless of where you live. IRS Pub 915 says SSA does not withhold US tax from a US citizen's benefits, and lists the countries whose residents are exempt from US tax on benefits (Canada, Egypt, Germany, Ireland, Israel, Italy for Italian citizens, Romania, United Kingdom). Portugal is not listed. Excluded foreign earned income is not subtracted when computing taxable benefits. For Portugal's side, see G2.
