A form attached to your tax return listing specified foreign financial assets. For people living abroad you file only above $200,000 (year-end) or $300,000 (any time) if single, or $400,000 / $600,000 if married filing jointly.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** In the US the figures are $50,000/$75,000 single and $100,000/$150,000 joint. "Abroad" means tax home in a foreign country and presence abroad at least 330 days in 12 consecutive months. Due with the return, including extensions. Form 8938 does not replace the FBAR; each has its own rules. Penalty: $10,000 plus $10,000 per 30 days after IRS notice, maximum $60,000 (comparison page); the FATCA summary page words it as $10,000 plus up to $50,000 for continued failure (same $60,000 total) and adds a 40% penalty on understatements tied to undisclosed assets. Neither page gives a general rule for foreign pension accounts, so that point is **REASONABLY TRUE / UNVERIFIED**; ask a professional.
