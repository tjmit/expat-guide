Ask your operator about notice and loyalty status, cancel in writing, in a shop, by phone or online, and expect an acknowledgement within 5 days. Complain via the Livro de Reclamações; ANACOM's line is 800 206 665.

## Citations and legal explanation

- If a cancellation request is incomplete the operator has 3 working days to say what is missing and you have 30 working days to complete it. Early exit during a loyalty period may cost a penalty or equipment return. Escalation: consumer arbitration centre, Julgado de Paz or courts.
