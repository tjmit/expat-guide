The lease must be in writing. A deposit is capped at two months' rent, and rent can be paid up to two months in advance if agreed in writing. You can block automatic renewal with 120, 90 or 60 days' notice depending on length, or leave early after one third of the term with 120 days' notice (60 days for terms under a year).

## Citations and legal explanation

- Art. 1069: the urban lease must be written. Art. 1076: advance rent up to two months by written agreement; guarantees up to two rents.
- Art. 1095: minimum term one year, but the minimum does not apply to non-permanent housing or transitional uses such as professional or education reasons.
- Art. 1098(1): tenant blocks renewal with 120 days (term of six years or more), 90 days (one to under six years), 60 days (six months to under one year), or one third of the term (under six months). Art. 1098(3): after one third of the term, tenant may terminate with 120 days (term of one year or more) or 60 days (under a year). Art. 1098(6): short notice does not stop termination but you owe rent for the missing notice period, unless due to involuntary unemployment, permanent incapacity or death.
- Art. 1077: if the lease is silent, rent can be updated yearly with at least 30 days' written notice using official coefficients.
