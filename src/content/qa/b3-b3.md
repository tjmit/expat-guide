It is a credit on your US return for income tax paid to Portugal, claimed on Form 1116. You cannot also take a credit for foreign tax on income you excluded.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** Pub 514 (2025): foreign income taxes taken as a credit reduce US tax liability. You cannot take a credit or deduction for foreign taxes paid on income you exclude under the FEIE, so you generally choose between excluding income and crediting its tax. Unused credit can be carried back one year and forward ten (summary of Pub 514, not verified verbatim). The small-amount election in the Form 1116 instructions (2025) lets you skip Form 1116 only if all foreign-source gross income was passive category income, creditable foreign taxes were not more than $300 ($600 joint), and all of it was reported on a qualified payee statement. Wages do not qualify. Which route is better depends on Portuguese tax rates; ask a professional.
