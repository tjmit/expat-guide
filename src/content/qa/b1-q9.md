Yes if you have lived in the US for over a year: an FBI police record, apostilled, issued less than 90 days before you submit the visa application.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** The checklist says the police clearance must be "issued less than 90 days before the submission of the visa application" and, separately, that if you resided in the USA for over a year it "must be issued by the FBI, duly apostilled (Hague Apostille)." The older one-pager gave no recency limit and allowed the record to be apostilled or "remain unopened in original sealed envelope"; the newer checklist supersedes it. Which date the 90 days is counted from, the FBI issue date or the apostille date: **[UNVERIFIED]**. Ask your VFS center, because a slow apostille (Q11) can use most of the window.
