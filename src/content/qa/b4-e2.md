Generally you must have held a valid permit for 2 years. Not for minors, a spouse who is the child’s parent, or family of Art. 90 (teaching or highly qualified) permit holders.

## Citations and legal explanation

Art. 98(1): a holder of a residence permit “válida há pelo menos dois anos” has the right to reunification, meaning held for two years, not issued for two years. Art. 98(2) shortens that period to 15 months for a spouse who cohabited at least 18 months before the holder entered Portugal. Art. 98(3) waives the wait for minors or dependent incapable family members, a spouse who is a parent of such a minor, and family of Art. 90, 90-A or 121-A permit holders. Art. 98(4) lets the minister waive or reduce it in exceptional cases. Per the notes on Art. 98, Lei 61/2025 (in force 2025-10-23) applies to procedures started after it entered into force.
