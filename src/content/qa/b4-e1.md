Yes. You can request reunification with your own permit application, and family can accompany a residence visa application.

## Citations and legal explanation

Art. 81(5): an applicant may request family reunification at the same time as the permit. Art. 58(5): the residence visa can also cover family members listed in Art. 99(1), applied for simultaneously. Art. 81(2): the request may extend to dependent minors.
