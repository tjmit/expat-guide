The State Dept fees page lists $130 for a renewal book, plus $60 for expedited service.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** Book and card $160; card only $30; expedited service $60; 1-2 day delivery $22.05 on the fees page (an older 2023 page says $19.53, so those conflict). The $35 acceptance fee applies only when applying in person. Eligibility rules (issued within 15 years, age 16 or older at issue) come from the 2023 renewal page (last updated September 26, 2023).
**Source:** https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/passports/how-apply/fees.html ; https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/passports/have-passport/renew.html
