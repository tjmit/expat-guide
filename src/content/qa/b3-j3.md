I could not find an official Portuguese or US rule or ruling on an employer applying Article 22 to its staff, on employer withholding duties when it does, or on penalties. Do not treat an employer's position as binding on your own filing duty.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** The Portuguese ruling treats the exemption as conditional on facts being met and does not discuss employer withholding or reporting. I found no ruling about private or international schools. A US university form (UC Office of the President, Portugal Article 22 statement) shows a mirror-image US practice: the individual signs a statement and the withholding agent relies on it, renewed each calendar year. That shows how an employer may handle it but is not Portuguese authority. Your obligation to file as a resident (J1) comes from your residence, not from your employer's treatment. Ask a Portuguese tax professional for a written view, or a binding ruling request (informação vinculativa) to the tax authority.
