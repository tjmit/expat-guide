Yes. Any foreigner with legal residence can get an SNS user number.

## Citations and legal explanation

gov.pt: the number is assigned the first time you visit a public health unit. For the SNS to cover costs, your registration must be linked to ID, a Portuguese NIF, a full Portuguese address and a valid residence permit. Art. 83(1)(e) of Lei 23/2007 lists access to health care among the rights of permit holders.
