No later than 30 days before expiry. You can start up to 90 days before. Renewal is requested on the AIMA renewals portal.


**UNVERIFIED:** IRN appointment status

## Citations and legal explanation

Art. 78(1) requires the request “até 30 dias antes de expirar a sua validade”. The gov.pt service page adds that you can apply from 90 days before expiry, and links the AIMA renewals portal. If the permit expired less than six months ago you can still renew. With no permit, or one expired for more than six months, you must file a new application at an AIMA counter. Per gov.pt, in-person renewal is by appointment only, and it is the IRN that contacts eligible people to book it. You do not book it yourself. The gov.pt page carries a notice that it may be out of date. Another guide in this project says IRN stopped renewal appointments on 1 Aug 2025. That conflicts with gov.pt and is not confirmed.
