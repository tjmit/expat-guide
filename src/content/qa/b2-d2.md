The US requires you to report foreign accounts: an FBAR if the combined value of foreign accounts exceeds 10,000 dollars at any time in the year, due 15 April with an automatic extension to 15 October. Whether Portuguese banks will make extra demands of US persons is unconfirmed.

## Citations and legal explanation

- IRS FBAR page (reviewed 30-Jul-2026): the threshold, April 15 due date, October 15 automatic extension, and electronic filing with FinCEN, not with your tax return.
- IRS FATCA page (reviewed 24-Sep-2026): FATCA requires foreign financial institutions to report assets held by their US account holders. The IRS pages do not say a Portuguese bank must collect a W-9 or refuse US persons.
