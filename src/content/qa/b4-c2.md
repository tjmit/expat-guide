It is in force. Lei Orgânica 1/2026, de 18 de maio, is marked “em vigor”.

## Citations and legal explanation

Approved 1 April 2026, promulgated 3 May 2026, referendada 4 May, published in Diário da República n.º 95/2026 on 18 May 2026, in force the next day (Art. 8). Declaração de Retificação 17/2026/1 was published the same day. In the consolidated Lei da Nacionalidade it corrects Art. 6(11): the criminal-record proof now refers to an effective prison sentence over 3 years, matching Art. 6(1)(f). It changes no residence period.
