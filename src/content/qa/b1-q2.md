A signed visa form, 2 photos, passport, travel medical insurance, an FBI police record (apostilled), a signed personal statement, proof of US address, proof of income, and one activity document such as a job contract or an invitation letter.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** From the 06.11.2025 checklist: passport valid at least 3 months beyond the intended stay; "Valid travel insurance, covering necessary medical expenses, including urgent medical assistance and possible repatriation"; police clearance (see Q9); personal statement with reason for settling, intended area and type of accommodation; "proof of source of income" (employment contract, promise of contract or service contract). Teaching/highly qualified activity: employment, promise or service contract; or an invitation letter from a higher education or professional training institution; or a sponsorship letter; or a cultural-activity or research-center invitation.
**Differences between the two VFS documents (the older, undated one-pager vs the 06.11.2025 checklist):** the one-pager asks for a contract of "minimum period of one year" and for 3 months of US bank or credit card statements, a proof of accommodation, and a request for a criminal record enquiry; the 06.11.2025 checklist says "at least six months" and lists none of those three items. Follow the newest checklist your VFS center gives you and ask the center in writing which applies.
**Salary thresholds (highly qualified salaried work):** 1.5 times the national average gross annual wage, or 3 times the IAS; or 1.2 times / 2 times IAS for professions in ISCO major groups 1 and 2 that a Council of Ministers resolution lists as particularly in need. Whether teaching is on that list: **[UNVERIFIED]**. Whether a K-12 school contract counts under the contract or invitation-letter options: **[UNVERIFIED]**.
