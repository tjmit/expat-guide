The State Dept's pages disagree, so the time is unknown: from 4 to 6 weeks on one page to 10 to 13 weeks on another.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** The processing-times page (last updated March 24, 2023) says routine 10 to 13 weeks, expedited 7 to 9 weeks. The fees page (October 9, 2025) shows a table with routine "4 to 6 weeks" and expedited "2 to 3 weeks." Mailing can add up to 2 weeks each way. Check the live page and renew early.
