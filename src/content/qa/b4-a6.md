Temporary permit: more than 6 consecutive months, or 8 months in total, can lead to cancellation. Permanent: 24 consecutive months, or 30 within 3 years.

## Citations and legal explanation

Art. 85(2) to (4). The rule applies to absences “sem razões atendíveis” (without valid reasons). Cancellation is a power, not automatic. You can justify an absence to AIMA before you leave, or exceptionally afterwards, and the permit is not cancelled if you carried out professional, business, cultural or social activity abroad. Relevant to teachers working elsewhere in summer and to retirees who travel.
