The law does not say the permit ends automatically. Renewal still requires you to meet the subsistence and housing conditions.


**UNVERIFIED:** Renewal after a contract ends

## Citations and legal explanation

Art. 85(1) lists cancellation grounds (expulsion, fraud, serious crime, public order, EU restrictive measures). Job loss is not listed. Lei 62/2026 amended Art. 85, and I read the current text. Art. 78(2) requires means of subsistence at renewal, and AIMA accepts pensions, financial income and other proofs. Whether an employer-category permit can be renewed on another basis, such as retirement income, was not verified.
