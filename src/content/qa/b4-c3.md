Language and culture proof, knowledge of rights and duties, a declaration of adherence to democratic principles, no serious conviction, and means of support.

## Citations and legal explanation

Art. 6(1)(c) to (i): proof by test or certificate of knowledge of Portuguese language, culture, history and national symbols; knowledge of fundamental rights and duties and state organization; a solemn declaration of adherence to democratic rule-of-law principles; no final prison sentence “efetiva” (actual, not suspended) over 3 years for terrorism, violent or highly organized crime, state-security crimes or aiding illegal immigration; not a danger to national security; not under UN or EU restrictive measures; ability to support yourself. The law gives no language level. Test details and proof of means are left to a regulation that I could not confirm as published (see C10).
