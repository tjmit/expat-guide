Register the change of ownership within 60 days of the sale.

## Citations and legal explanation

- Online (Automóvel Online), at a registry office or Loja do Cidadão. For a car imported from abroad the 60 days run from the plate assignment. Taxes named: ISV (registration) and IUC (annual); rates not stated on the page. People moving their residence to Portugal may request an ISV exemption within 12 months, subject to conditions on the page. Insurance obligation and document list: [UNVERIFIED].
