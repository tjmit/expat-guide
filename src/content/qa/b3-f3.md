Not confirmed. The treaty says pensions for past employment are taxable only in the residence State, but whether and how a US plan or IRA withdrawal fits that wording, and how Portugal taxes it, needs a professional.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** Treaty Article 20(1)(a) (verbatim): "pensions and other similar remuneration derived and beneficially owned by a resident of a Contracting State in consideration of past employment shall be taxable only in that State". The US saving clause still lets the US tax its citizens (see G3), with relief under Article 25 (see G5).
