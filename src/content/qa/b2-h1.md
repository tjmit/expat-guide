Schooling is compulsory from ages 6 to 18; enrol through the Portal das Matrículas or at the school for your area.

## Citations and legal explanation

- Children turning 6 by 15 September start 1.º ano. Pre-school (3 to 6) is voluntary. Newly arrived students may take Portuguese as a non-mother tongue. Foreign school records need an equivalence process with authenticated, apostilled and officially translated documents.
