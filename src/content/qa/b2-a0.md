Three different numbers do three different jobs. The **NIF** is your Portuguese tax number, from the tax authority. It is the one that compares to the US Social Security number when you use it as your tax number, for example on a tax return. The **NISS** is your Portuguese Social Security number, from Segurança Social, Portugal's Social Security agency. It is your account number for contributions and benefits. Portugal uses two separate numbers for these two jobs. The **SNS number**, called the **número de utente** (a "utente" is a user or patient of the health service), is your public health care number. Portugal's Social Security agency also covers unemployment, sickness, parental and family benefits, not only pensions, but not health care, which is the separate SNS (see the last bullet below). ## Citations and legal explanation

- **NIF (número de identificação fiscal), also called número de contribuinte:** issued by the tax authority (AT). gov.pt says it "permite trabalhar, abrir uma conta bancária ou cumprir as obrigações fiscais" (lets you work, open a bank account or meet your tax obligations) and is needed to buy goods or services and sign contracts. Foreigners, resident or not, who work and pay tax in Portugal need one. **US comparison:** the Social Security number as your tax number, the one you put on tax forms and give a bank. [UNVERIFIED] The comparison is ours.
- **NISS (número de identificação de segurança social):** issued by Segurança Social, Portugal's Social Security agency. gov.pt says it "permite aceder a direitos, como apoios e subsídios, e cumprir deveres relativos a contribuições" (gives access to rights such as support and subsidies, and lets you meet contribution duties). Employers can request it for employees. **US comparison:** the Social Security account side of the US SSN, which Portugal keeps as a separate number from the NIF. It is not your tax number. [UNVERIFIED] The comparison is ours.
- **SNS number (número de utente):** issued by the central health administration (ACSS). gov.pt says it is for "quem precise de cuidados de saúde nas unidades públicas do SNS" (anyone who needs care at public SNS facilities). Foreigners get it the first time they go to a public health unit such as a health centre or hospital. gov.pt also says having the number "não garante a cobertura das despesas" (does not guarantee that the SNS covers your costs). **US comparison:** like a health plan member ID. [UNVERIFIED] The comparison is Claude's own, with no source.
- **What Portuguese Social Security covers, and what it does not:** a 2018 US Social Security Administration description of Portugal, an older source, lists old-age, disability and survivors pensions, a cash sickness benefit, unemployment benefits, maternity and paternity benefits and family allowances. Health care (doctors and hospitals) is provided by the SNS, per gov.pt, which is a separate system. The two meet at the European Health Insurance Card, which requires registration with both (see the EU health card answer below).
- **All three at once:** gov.pt says foreigners with a Portuguese address can request the NIF, NISS and SNS number together. They are free. The pages do not say whether the numbers are lifelong, and another answer here says they do not expire. See the answers below for how to get each one and how to look them up later.
