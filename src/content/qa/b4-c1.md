10 years for US citizens. 7 years for EU and Portuguese-speaking-country nationals.

## Citations and legal explanation

Art. 6(1)(b) of the Lei da Nacionalidade as amended: legal residence of at least seven years for nationals of Portuguese-speaking countries and EU member states, or 10 years for nationals of other countries. Separate periods of legal residence add up if they fall within a maximum window of 12 years for non-EU, non-CPLP nationals (Art. 15(3)). The consolidated text still reads this way after the later rectification.
