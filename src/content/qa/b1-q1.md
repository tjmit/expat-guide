It depends on your US state of residence. Washington DC, New York and San Francisco use VFS Global centers; Boston, New Bedford, Newark and Providence are listed as consulates general, not VFS.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** VFS requires an appointment at the center "that has jurisdiction over their place of residence." From the Apr-2026 PDF: Washington DC (also Houston and Miami centers): Alabama, Arkansas, Florida, Georgia, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maryland, Minnesota, Mississippi, Missouri, Nebraska, North Carolina, North Dakota, Ohio, Oklahoma, South Carolina, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Virginia, West Virginia, Wisconsin, DC. New York: Michigan, New York, Connecticut. San Francisco: Alaska, Arizona, California, Colorado, and the list is cut off in the text read. Boston Consulate General: Massachusetts (except the New Bedford area), Maine, New Hampshire, Vermont. Newark Consulate General: Delaware, New Jersey, Pennsylvania. Providence: Rhode Island. Hawaii, Idaho, Montana, Nevada, New Mexico, Oregon, Utah, Washington and Wyoming did not appear in the extracted text. **[UNVERIFIED]** Which of those go to San Francisco: read the PDF itself. Proof of US address (lease and/or driver's license) is required "for jurisdiction confirmation purposes."
