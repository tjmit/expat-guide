You file at an AIMA store ("Loja AIMA") by appointment, or on an electronic platform AIMA is rolling out for visa holders, with the signed Modelo 1 form, your passport, your valid residence visa and supporting documents. AIMA must decide within 90 days, extendable by 30.

## Citations and legal explanation

- **Teaching permit (art. 90):** AIMA lists: valid passport; residence visa valid for teaching; criminal record certificate; sworn address declaration with a landlord statement if you rent; tax registration; Social Security registration; and an employer declaration of a compatible contract, or an invitation letter from a school, training centre or research centre. The request can be made by you or by the host institution. The AIMA page says a permit under this article is valid two years, renewable in three-year periods.
- **Highly qualified activity (art. 90):** same documents plus proof of qualification; for employees, a contract with minimum pay of 1.5 times the average national gross annual salary (AIMA page states 2,157.00 euros/month) or three times the IAS, with the reference values dated 2023 and 2024.
- **Law:** Lei 23/2007 art. 90(1) grants the permit for teaching, highly qualified or cultural activity. Art. 90(2) waives the residence visa if you entered and stayed legally. Art. 82(5): decision within 90 days, extendable by 30 in exceptional, justified cases.
- **Visa length:** gov.pt visa guide: the visa for obtaining a residence permit is valid for two entries and four months. Page dated 02/09/2026 and still uses SEF wording. Rated PROBABLY TRUE for current practice.
- **AIMA contact centre:** (+351) 217 115 000, Monday to Friday 8:00 to 20:00 (AIMA page).
