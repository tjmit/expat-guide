Yes, you can stay. The renewal receipt has the same effect as your residence card for 60 days, and that period is renewable. Working on it is a reasonable reading, not stated in the article.


**UNVERIFIED:** Repeal of Art. 78(6), End of automatic extensions

## Citations and legal explanation

Art. 78(7) says the receipt produces the same effects as the residence title for 60 days, renewable. The text does not say how often it can be renewed or how long AIMA takes. Art. 78(6) now reads “[Revogado]” and Lei 62/2026 is listed as having amended Art. 78. I could not identify what Art. 78(6) said, when it was repealed or by which act. A private blog says automatic extension of expired cards “ended”. No official source confirms that, so this guide does not rely on it.
