You can choose any supplier and switch at any time at no cost; the new supplier handles cancelling the old one, within about three weeks, without cutting your supply.

## Citations and legal explanation

- A social tariff applies automatically to qualifying households; gov.pt gives an income threshold of 5,808 euros a year plus 2,904 euros per non-earning member.
