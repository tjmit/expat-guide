A temporary permit for teaching or highly qualified activity under Art. 90.

## Citations and legal explanation

Art. 90(1): available for teaching at a higher-education institution or an education or vocational establishment, with the Art. 77 general conditions plus the listed items (a compatible contract or service contract, an invitation letter, a certified company’s statement, or a recognised cultural project). Read the list as alternatives alongside Art. 77, and have a lawyer confirm. Art. 90(2): no visa needed if you entered and stayed legally. Art. 77(1) conditions include a valid visa (or a job-search visa), subsistence, housing, social-security registration where applicable, no conviction above one year and no SIS entry.
