Means of subsistence, housing, tax and social-security compliance, and no serious criminal record.

## Citations and legal explanation

Art. 78(2): renewal only if you (a) have means of subsistence as set by ministerial order, (b) have housing, (c) are up to date with tax and social security, and (d) have no sentence or sentences exceeding one year of prison. Art. 78(3) allows refusal for public order or security reasons. Art. 78(4): an illness appearing after your first permit is not grounds for refusal. Art. 78(5): no renewal for a person declared “contumaz” until the declaration lapses. Art. 77(3) allows refusal or non-renewal for EU restrictive measures. Art. 81(3) requires refusal of an application or renewal for an SIS entry, a prison sentence over one year, more than one conviction of that kind, or UCFE security findings. Lei 62/2026 amended Arts. 78 and 81 (in force 2026-09-11). I read the current text and did not compare versions.
