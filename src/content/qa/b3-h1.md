Yes. Use the Federal Post Card Application (FPCA) to register and request an absentee ballot, and renew it each year.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** The FPCA registers you, requests a ballot and updates contact information; FVAP recommends submitting it every year or when your contact details change. Your voting residence is the state where you last lived before leaving the US, even without current ties there. If you have not received your ballot 30 days before the election, FVAP recommends submitting the Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB). State return methods (email, fax) vary; check the current 2026-27 Voting Assistance Guide for your state. Voting in a state can be evidence of domicile for state tax purposes (see D1; confirmed only in the Virginia ruling); ask a professional before choosing a voting state.
