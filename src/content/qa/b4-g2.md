For some steps, yes. EU long-term resident status requires health insurance, or proof of SNS coverage on AIMA’s list. Other permits were not checked.


**UNVERIFIED:** Insurance rules for other permits

## Citations and legal explanation

AIMA’s long-term resident page asks for a health insurance contract or proof of SNS coverage. Art. 52(1)(f) requires travel insurance for visas. Insurance rules for other permit types and for retirees were not verified.
