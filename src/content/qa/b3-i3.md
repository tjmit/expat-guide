A 20% flat rate on Portuguese employment and self-employment income for 10 years, for people in listed qualified activities who were not Portuguese tax residents in the previous five years.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** Art. 58.º-A EBF (added by Lei 82/2023), implemented by Portaria 352/2024/1. n.º 1: resident under art. 16 CIRS, not resident in any of the five previous years, carrying on activities in alíneas a) to g): a) higher-education teaching and scientific research; b) qualified posts under contractual investment benefits; c) highly qualified professions listed by portaria; d) other qualified posts recognised by AICEP or IAPMEI; e) R&D personnel; f) startup posts; g) Azores and Madeira. n.º 2: 20% special rate on net category A and B income for 10 consecutive years from registration as resident. Anyone who benefited from NHR is excluded; usable once. Application by 15 January of the year after becoming resident. Foreign-source income is generally exempt, except category H (pensions), per the official FAQ (FAQ text, not statute); low-tax-jurisdiction dividends at 35%.
