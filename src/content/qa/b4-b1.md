After 5 years on a temporary permit, with a clean record, means of subsistence, housing and basic Portuguese.

## Citations and legal explanation

Art. 80(1) of Lei 23/2007: temporary permit for at least 5 years; no sentence over one year of prison in the last 5 years of residence; means of subsistence; housing; “português básico”. AIMA’s page repeats these and adds tax and social-security compliance, which is not in the text of Art. 80(1). The law and AIMA say “basic Portuguese”. They do not state a CEFR level. A2 appears on AIMA’s page only as one accepted certificate type.
