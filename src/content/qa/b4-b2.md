Two steps: request an appointment through AIMA’s contact form, then apply in person with the paperwork.


**UNVERIFIED:** Fee amounts

## Citations and legal explanation

AIMA says the appointment request does not replace the application. Accepted proof of basic Portuguese: a Portuguese school certificate, a certificate from a Portuguese-speaking country, an IEFP basic Portuguese certificate, a recognised school’s basic Portuguese certificate, a CAPLE test certificate, or an A2-or-higher Português Língua de Acolhimento certificate. Housing is proven by an honor declaration plus a land-registry document if you own, or a landlord declaration if you rent. Fees follow the AIMA table (see A5).
