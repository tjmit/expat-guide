The employer can file the permit application. For employment permits under Art. 88, a lawful contract and social-security registration are required.


**UNVERIFIED:** Art. 88 for Art. 90 permits

## Citations and legal explanation

Art. 81(1): the application can be filed by you, a legal representative, or the employer. Art. 88(1) (general employment permits) requires a lawful contract and social-security registration, and AIMA notifies the labour inspectorate, tax authority and social security of grants (Art. 88(4)). Whether Art. 88 applies in full to Art. 90 teaching permits is not stated in the text I read.
