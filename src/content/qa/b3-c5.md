Possibly. A fund or ETF not domiciled in the US may trigger Form 8621 (PFIC rules).

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** Pub 54: "If you are an individual taxpayer that owns a mutual fund or ETF that is not domiciled in the United States, you may have a Form 8621 obligation." Pub 54 also mentions Form 3520 for certain foreign trusts and foreign gifts. Ask a professional about any non-US fund.
