Yes. A residence visa for teaching in an educational or vocational institution.


**UNVERIFIED:** AIMA visa code for teachers

## Citations and legal explanation

Art. 61(1) of Lei 23/2007 covers teaching, highly qualified or cultural activity, without prejudice to the EU Blue Card regime. You need the general Art. 52 conditions plus one of: an employment contract or promise of one, or a service contract; an invitation letter from an educational or vocational institution; a certified company’s responsibility statement; a cultural-activity invitation; or a research-centre invitation. The decision deadline is 30 days (Art. 61(3)) and the general Art. 59 regime does not apply to these applicants (Art. 61(4)). A residence visa is valid for two entries and 4 months (Art. 58(2)) and comes with a pre-authorization of residence and provisional tax, social-security and health numbers (Art. 58(6)). Which visa code AIMA assigns to teachers was not checked.
