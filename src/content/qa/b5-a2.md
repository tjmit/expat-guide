CIPLE is the Initial Certificate of Portuguese as a Foreign Language, level A2, run by CAPLE at the University of Lisbon. The organiser's page lists a 2026 exam fee of €95 and four sample files you can try to check your level (see the buttons above). The page does not say the samples are free, so confirm before you rely on that.

## Citations and legal explanation

- **Sample material (from CAPLE's CIPLE page):** reading and writing paper, listening paper, listening audio and an answer key. The links were taken from the page and the files were not opened here. There is no speaking sample and no full past papers.
- **Structure (CAPLE page, paraphrased):** reading and writing, 1 hour 15 minutes, 45%. Listening, 30 minutes, 30%. Speaking, 15 minutes per pair of candidates, 25%. The page's own "number of components" field is inconsistent with its text.
- **Grading:** 55% to 100% passes. Suficiente 55 to 69%, Bom 70 to 84%, Muito Bom 85 to 100%.
- **Fee and sittings:** €95 per the CAPLE page and the University of Algarve exam centre page (an older 2024 article said €79). International sessions are in May, July and November. The 2026 calendar was only an image and could not be read.
- **Citizenship:** one article says you generally need A2 for citizenship but cites no law. Our answer on the level of Portuguese for citizenship found that the law names no level. [UNVERIFIED] Whether CIPLE is accepted. Check before you pay for an exam.
