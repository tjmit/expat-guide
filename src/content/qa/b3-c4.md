Foreign financial institutions are required to report accounts of US taxpayers under FATCA, so you should assume your Portuguese accounts can reach the IRS.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** The IRS says FATCA requires certain foreign financial institutions to report directly to the IRS on accounts held by US taxpayers. Treasury lists a Portugal FATCA agreement (dated 2015); its terms were not read.
