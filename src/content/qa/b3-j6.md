Progressive, from 12.5% on the first slice to 48% above EUR 86,634 of taxable income, plus a solidarity surcharge above EUR 80,000.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** Art. 68 CIRS table for 2026, column "A": up to 8,342: 12.5%; to 12,587: 15.7%; to 17,838: 21.2%; to 23,089: 24.1%; to 29,397: 31.1%; to 43,090: 34.9%; to 46,566: 43.1%; to 86,634: 44.6%; above: 48%. The leaflet also prints a second column "B" and does not explain it in the part I read; I use column A. Joint filing applies the rates to half of taxable income. Solidarity surcharge (mainland): 2.5% on 80,000 to 250,000; 5% above. The AT newsletter (Jan 2026) adds: brackets updated by 3.51%, specific deduction EUR 4,587.09, "mínimo de existência" EUR 12,880. Withholding tables were not read.
