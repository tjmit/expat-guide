The fine for late or missing filing under the general tax-offences law is EUR 150 to EUR 3,750, and it can be reduced if you file before a process starts.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** RGIT art. 116 n.º 1 (verbatim): "é punível com coima de € 150 a € 3750." Art. 116 n.º 3: the fine does not apply if you were exempt from filing for that year. The explainer (secondary) says the fine can drop to 12.5% of the legal minimum but not below EUR 25 if you file before a process starts; that a late substitute return can carry fines of EUR 93.75 to EUR 5,625 (no tax due) or EUR 375 to EUR 22,500 (with more tax due); and that late filing can cost joint taxation, some benefits and part of a refund. These secondary points were not checked against the articles.
