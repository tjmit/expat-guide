It lets qualifying people exclude foreign earned income from US income tax up to a yearly cap: $130,000 for 2025 and $132,900 for 2026, per person.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** Claimed on Form 2555. The IRS gives $260,000 combined for 2025 for couples who both qualify; the page gives no 2026 couple figure. The cap is prorated by qualifying days in a part-year. Housing: IRS gives a limit of $39,000 for 2025 and $39,870 for 2026. The exclusion reduces regular income tax but not self-employment tax. The main FEIE page still shows only older-year amounts (2020 to 2023); use the "figuring" page.
