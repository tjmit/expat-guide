Rent is the biggest difference between these places, and the published figures only cover some of them. The median rent on new leases signed in 2025 was about €16.88 per square metre in Lisbon, €16.20 in Cascais and €15.03 in Oeiras, against €9.29 across Portugal. Estoril and Carcavelos sit inside the Cascais council area, so the Cascais figure is the nearest number for them. No Sintra figure was found.

## Citations and legal explanation

- **What the figures are:** the report says the national median for the 149,628 new residential lease contracts signed in 2025 was €9.29 per square metre, up 9.7% on the previous year, and attributes this to the National Statistics Institute (INE). Lisbon, Cascais and Oeiras are named as the three council areas above €15 per square metre. Greater Lisbon is given as €14.19. [UNVERIFIED] The INE table was not read.
- **Estoril and Carcavelos:** they are parishes (freguesias, roughly like districts or neighborhoods) of the Cascais council area (Cascais e Estoril, and Carcavelos e Parede). The report gives no parish figures, so rents there may sit above or below the Cascais figure. [UNVERIFIED]
- **Sintra:** not mentioned in the report. No figure is given here.
- **Worked example, not a listing:** at the Cascais median, a 70 square metre home would be about €1,134 a month (70 × €16.20). That is arithmetic on a median, not a price you will find advertised.
- **Not covered:** other living costs (groceries, utilities, transport, dining). No sourced comparison was found, so none is given.
**Sources:** [The Portugal News, 17 July 2026](https://www.theportugalnews.com/news/2026-07-17/median-housing-rent-rises-in-2025-to-929-m-in-portugal/1056769), reporting INE.
