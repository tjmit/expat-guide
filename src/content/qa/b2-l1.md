Three years of legal guarantee on movable goods from a professional seller, and 14 days to withdraw from distance purchases.

## Citations and legal explanation

- Decreto-Lei 84/2021: 3 years for movable goods; defects in the first 2 years presumed present at delivery; repair or replacement within about 30 days; used goods can be reduced to 18 months by agreement. The 14-day withdrawal exceptions (custom-made, opened audio/video/software) come from a partial summary of the Your Europe page.
- Consumer authority DGC: 213 564 600, weekdays 10:00 to 13:00.
