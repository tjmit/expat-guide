Yes, if your gross income reaches the filing threshold. US citizens are taxed on worldwide income wherever they live.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** The IRS FAQ states that worldwide income is subject to US income tax regardless of where you live, and that citizens must file while living and working abroad. You must file for any year in which gross income is at least the standard deduction. The page does not give the dollar thresholds; it points to the "Do You Have to File?" section of the Form 1040 Instructions. The FAQ also says you generally need to file returns going back six years if you are behind. Filing is still required even when the foreign earned income exclusion or foreign tax credit removes the tax (IRS 2023 tip, dated but consistent).
**Source:** https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-about-international-individual-tax-matters
