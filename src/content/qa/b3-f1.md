Not to the extent your pay is excluded. Excluded foreign earned income is not IRA "compensation".

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** Pub 590-A, "What Isn't Compensation?": "Any amounts (other than combat pay) you exclude from income, such as foreign earned income and housing costs." IRA limit: $7,000 ($8,000 age 50+) for 2025; $7,500 ($8,600 age 50+) for 2026 (Pub 590-A "What's New"; IRS Notice 2025-67 gives the same $7,500 and a $1,100 age-50 catch-up, which adds up to $8,600).
**Sources:** https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p590a.pdf ; https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-25-67.pdf
