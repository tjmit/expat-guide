Legal residence means being regularised under any title, visa or authorization of the foreigners or asylum regimes. The law does not say whether time on a pending application counts, or name a start date.


**UNVERIFIED:** Pending-application time, Clock start date

## Citations and legal explanation

Art. 15(1): residing legally means being in Portugal with your situation regularised “ao abrigo de qualquer dos títulos, vistos ou autorizações” under the foreigners and asylum regimes. Art. 15(3) lets separate periods add up within a 12-year window for non-EU, non-CPLP nationals. Nothing in the text says whether time while an application is pending counts, or fixes when the 7 or 10 year clock starts. Secondary sources disagree. Do not rely on a start date without legal advice.
