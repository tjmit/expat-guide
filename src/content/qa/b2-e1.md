It is free. Foreigners without a Portuguese Citizen Card activate it in person at a service counter, with a passport or residence document; residence documents require a Portuguese NIF.

## Citations and legal explanation

- Online, video-call and app activation are limited to holders of the Portuguese Citizen Card. Each holder needs their own mobile number. For passports and residence documents, the CMD is valid as long as the linked document. Counter locator page not opened.
