The US gives a credit for Portuguese income tax, and Portugal gives a deduction for US tax paid on income the US may tax, with special rules for US citizens living in Portugal.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** Article 25(1): the US "shall allow to a resident or citizen of the United States as a credit" the income tax paid to Portugal, subject to US law limits. Article 25(2): for a US citizen resident in Portugal, income the US may tax solely because of citizenship is treated as arising in Portugal for credit purposes, provided the US tax is not less than it would otherwise be. Article 25(3)(a): Portugal allows a deduction from its tax equal to income tax paid in the US on income the US may tax under the treaty. Article 25 is outside the saving clause (G3).
