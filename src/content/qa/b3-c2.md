Civil penalties can be up to $16,536 per non-willful violation and up to $165,353 (or 50% of the account balance, if greater) for willful violations, for penalties assessed on or after 17 January 2025.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** Pub 5569 says the civil amounts are set by 31 CFR 1010.821 and adjusted annually: non-willful up to the table amount; willful up to the greater of the table amount or 50% of the account balance at the time of the violation. The independent review reports the table values above (eCFR current to 23 Oct 2026, 90 FR 5630). Amounts change every year; check the current table. Criminal penalties can also apply. Pre-2016 figures ($10,000; greater of $100,000 or 50%) are historical only.
