No, not to get the NIF itself. You may need one, or an electronic notification channel, within 15 days of creating a tax relationship in Portugal, and non-EU self-employed people must appoint one.

## Citations and legal explanation

- AT: non-residents can request a NIF without a representative. Triggers for a representative or notification channel: owning a registered vehicle or real estate, an employment contract, or self-employment. Third-country nationals with self-employment in Portugal must appoint a representative.
- ePortugal's non-resident document list says a representative is needed, which appears to be an older process. AT's page is the authority.
