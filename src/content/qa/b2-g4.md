MobiCascais is the Cascais council's transport system: council buses, a free app, parking and bike sharing. People who live, study or work in Cascais ride the council buses free with a Viver Cascais card, on routes inside the Cascais area. You can join online or at a Loja Cascais (a local-government service office). The pages do not say what documents you need or whether the physical card costs anything, so ask the council first.

## Citations and legal explanation

- **Who rides free:** the council's transport page says council routes are free "for those who live, study or work in Cascais", and the mobility page says residents, workers and students need a valid Viver Cascais card. Visitors are not listed. Status must first be proven.
- **What is covered:** 44 council bus lines serving all four parishes (freguesias, roughly districts or neighborhoods: Alcabideche, Carcavelos e Parede, Cascais e Estoril, São Domingos de Rana). Trips beyond the Cascais area are not free. [UNVERIFIED] Whether the free rule covers Scotturb or CP train services is not stated.
- **How to join:** online or at the Lojas Cascais. There is a digital card in the free MobiCascais app (iOS and Android), validated by QR code on the bus. Registration is from age 18, and a parent or guardian can register a minor. Help line: 800 203 186 (from the MobiCascais FAQ).
- **Not stated on any page read:** required documents, cost of the physical card, processing time, whether foreign residents have extra rules, and Loja addresses. [UNVERIFIED]
- **Other services:** the app also shows timetables, car park availability and EV charging points, and kiosks rent bicycles and scooters. The bike sharing status is [UNVERIFIED] (one page dates from 2020).
