## Citations and legal explanation

AIMA’s page ties the amount to the minimum wage (Portaria 1563/2007) and cites Decreto-Lei 112/2024 for the 2025 figure. For family reunification it uses 100% of the minimum wage for the first adult, 50% for each further adult, and 30% for each child under 18 or dependent adult child. A pension is accepted proof when issued by the paying country’s official body with an authenticated declaration. The 920-euro figure for 2026 was announced by the Government and reported as published in Diário da República on 29 Dec 2025 (Decreto-Lei 139/2025 appears in the DR file index for that date).
