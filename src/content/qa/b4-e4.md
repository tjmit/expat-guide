Adequate housing and enough income to support everyone without social benefits.

## Citations and legal explanation

Art. 101(1). Family members must take Portuguese-language and constitutional-values integration measures and, for minors, compulsory schooling. Renewal of a family permit depends on compliance (Art. 101(3) and (4)). Income percentages are in B5. The decision deadline is nine months (Art. 105(1)). The exceptional extension in Art. 105(2) does not apply to the cases in Art. 98(1) and (2).
