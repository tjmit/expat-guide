Yes. Banks must offer a minimum banking services account to any individual who holds no other demand deposit account. Fees are capped at 1% of the IAS a year, about 5.37 euros in 2026.

## Citations and legal explanation

- Decreto-Lei 107/2017 (which amends Decreto-Lei 27-C/2000) states banks may not ask for documents beyond those needed for an ordinary demand account, may not require a minimum deposit or other products, and may not offer overdrafts. Its preamble keeps the cap on charges at 1% of the IAS; 1% of 537.13 euros is 5.37 euros (my arithmetic).
- Closure grounds (art. 5): unlawful use, no payment operations for 24 consecutive months, false information, ceasing to be legally resident in the EU, or holding another account. Closure for the last three grounds takes effect 60 days after notice.
- PROBABLY TRUE: the bank must respond within 10 working days, and the account includes 48 interbank transfers a year through homebanking. Both come from the Banco de Portugal brochure read through our tools and a review check; Banco de Portugal's site sits behind a bot check, so we did not reread it.
