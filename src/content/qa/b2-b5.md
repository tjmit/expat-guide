Foreign nationals with legal residence receive it at their first visit to a public health unit (health centre or hospital).

## Citations and legal explanation

- For SNS cover, the record should link ID, a Portuguese NIF, a full Portuguese address and a valid residence authorisation. Having the number does not by itself guarantee coverage. Full registration document list: [UNVERIFIED].
