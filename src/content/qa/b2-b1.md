In person at a Finanças office or Loja do Cidadão by appointment, with your passport (and entry visa) and proof of your foreign address. The online route is only through a legal representative.

## Citations and legal explanation

- AT: applicants need a civil ID document such as a passport, including a valid entry visa if arriving from a third country, plus a document showing their address abroad unless it is on the ID. Foreign-language documents need certified translations. Online requests go through e-balcão and must be submitted by a legal representative. Appointments: AT contact centre (+351) 217 206 707, working days 9:00 to 19:00.
- A NIF issued to a non-resident has non-resident status; update the address and status when you become tax resident.
- ePortugal says the NIF is free of charge. PROBABLY TRUE: the AT page does not state it.
