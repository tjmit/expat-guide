FinCEN Form 114, filed electronically, if the combined value of your foreign financial accounts exceeded $10,000 at any time in the year. Due April 15, with an automatic extension to October 15.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** Filed through FinCEN's BSA E-Filing System, not with your tax return. Covers bank, brokerage and mutual-fund accounts abroad, whether or not they earn taxable income. The IRS page excludes accounts in an IRA you own and retirement plan accounts where you are a participant or beneficiary (US plans). Keep records for five years.
