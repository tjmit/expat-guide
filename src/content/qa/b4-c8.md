Portuguese naturalization does not require renunciation. What the US requires is not covered here.


**UNVERIFIED:** US-side rules

## Citations and legal explanation

Art. 6(1) lists no renunciation requirement. Art. 8 of the Lei da Nacionalidade provides for loss of Portuguese nationality only when a national of another state declares they do not want to be Portuguese. US-side rules were not researched.
