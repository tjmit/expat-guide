Yes. Married more than 3 years to a Portuguese national, or in a recognised de facto union for more than 3 years.

## Citations and legal explanation

Art. 3(1), (3) and (4) as amended. For a de facto union, a court must first recognise it. Opposition is not possible once the marriage or union exceeds 6 years or there are Portuguese children, except on the criminal and security grounds in Art. 6(1)(f) to (h) (Art. 9(2)). This route has no 10-year requirement.
