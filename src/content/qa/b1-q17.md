Yes for ordinary items, with restrictions; not for prescription medicine. Delivery, signature and return rules were not found on the official pages (NOT CONFIRMED), so check with the carrier you use.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Not found on the official pages read:** whether a signature is required for a given service, and the redelivery and return rules. **[UNVERIFIED]**
**Details (USPS International Mail Manual, page undated):** prohibited items include arms and weapons, butane lighters, valuables such as jewelry, coins and banknotes, live animals and plants, tobacco. Import permits are needed for shipments Portugal regards as commercial. Personal shipments valued at $300 or more need a duplicate invoice. Priority Mail International 66 lb limit; First-Class Package International 4 lb limit. "Global Express Guaranteed service is suspended as of September 29, 2024." "The VAT exemption on imported goods was abolished as of July 1, 2021." The State Dept Portugal page says Portuguese law prohibits mailing prescription medicines and such parcels "will be impounded."
