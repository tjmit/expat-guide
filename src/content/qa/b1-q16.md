The EU has a duty relief for personal property of people moving residence from outside the EU. It applies to non-Portuguese people too, subject to conditions. VAT relief and Portugal's exact procedure are not confirmed.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:**
- **EU duty relief (Regulation (EC) No 1186/2009, as reported by the independent fact-check):** applies to "natural persons transferring their normal place of residence from a third country" into the EU; normal residence outside the EU for at least 12 months (Art. 5); goods owned and used at least 6 months before the move (Art. 4(a)); intended for the same use at the new home (Art. 4(b)); imported within 12 months of establishing residence (Art. 7(1)); no relief for alcohol, tobacco, commercial vehicles or tools of a trade (Art. 6); no lending, hiring out or transfer for 12 months (Art. 8). The text was not read by the author of this answer; verify the articles on EUR-Lex.
- **Portuguese customs codes:** the AT list has code C01, "Bens pessoais importados por pessoas singulares que transferem a sua residência habitual para o território aduaneiro da União," legal basis Reg. (EC) 1186/2009 art. 3; C42 and C43 cover release before residence is established or intent to establish it, with a commitment.
- **VAT:** the AT VAT-exemption table has code 417 for the same persons under Decreto-Lei 31/89 arts. 2 to 10. The conditions (residence period, ownership period, time limit) are not in the table and the Diário da República text could not be read. **[UNVERIFIED]**
- **Vehicle tax (ISV):** the gov.pt page (updated 10.11.2026) says people who move their legal residence to Portugal may be exempt from ISV if they: are over 18; lived abroad at least 6 months; owned the vehicle at least 6 months before the move; bought the vehicle where they lived and paid its taxes; and request the exemption within 12 months of transferring residence, online at the Portal das Finanças; one exemption per person. The page sets no nationality condition, which is our reading, not an express statement.
- **Portuguese-citizens page:** the gov.pt page on importing goods (updated 27.03.2026 per the reviewer) is written for Portuguese citizens returning from abroad (one year of residence outside the EU, six months' prior use of goods). Do not assume it applies to you.
- Ask a customs broker or the AT before you ship.
