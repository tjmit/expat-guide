The landlord must report every lease to AT by the end of the month after it starts, and stamp duty of 10% of the monthly rent applies.

## Citations and legal explanation

- Report via Portal das Finanças (Contratos, Comunicar contrato) or Modelo 2. Changes and termination are reported by the end of the following month.
