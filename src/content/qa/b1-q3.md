VFS lists $127.11 for residency visas plus a $43.91 VFS service fee. The legal deadline to decide a residence visa application is 60 days.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** New York, Washington DC, Miami and Houston centers take the fees by card at VFS. The undated D3 one-pager says San Francisco and Washington DC pay the visa fee by money order or cashier's check to the "Consulate General of Portugal," while the VFS index page says Washington DC pays by card; the VFS documents conflict, so ask your center. Decision deadline: "60 days" for a resident visa. The visa allows two entries and is valid for four months, during which you must apply for a residence permit with AIMA.
