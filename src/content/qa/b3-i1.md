If you stay more than 183 days (consecutive or not) in any 12-month period starting or ending in the tax year, or you have a home there you clearly intend as your habitual residence on any day of that period.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** Residents typically pay tax on worldwide income; non-residents only on Portuguese-source income. You must update your status by updating your address with the tax authority within 60 days. Third-country nationals use ID plus an AIMA document. The treaty tie-breaker (G1) matters only if both countries treat you as resident.
