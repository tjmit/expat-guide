April 15, but if you live outside the US you get an automatic extension to June 15 to file and pay. A further extension to October 15 is possible. Interest still runs from April 15.

## Citations and legal explanation

**Details:** To qualify, on the regular due date you must be living outside the US and Puerto Rico with your main place of business or post of duty outside them (or be military abroad). The IRS FAQ says to attach a statement to the return explaining which situation qualifies you. The additional four months (six in total, to October 15) must be requested by June 15. An extension to file is not an extension to pay; interest and late-payment penalties run from the original due date. Pub 54 also describes a discretionary extra extension to December 15 by letter sent by October 15, and Form 2350 for people who need more time to meet the FEIE residency tests. Pub 54 names Form 4868 as the six-month extension form.
