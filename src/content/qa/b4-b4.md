A separate status after 5 years of legal, uninterrupted residence. The statute requires health insurance. AIMA accepts an insurance contract or proof of SNS coverage.


**UNVERIFIED:** Practical benefits of the EU status

## Citations and legal explanation

Art. 126(1): 5 years of legal uninterrupted residence immediately before applying; stable and regular resources; “seguro de saúde”; housing; basic Portuguese. Absences do not break the period if each is under 6 consecutive months and the total is no more than 10 months (Art. 126(4)). AIMA’s document list accepts a health insurance contract or proof of SNS coverage. AIMA says the status is permanent and the EU card is valid at least 5 years and renewable. What it adds in practice for a US citizen is not covered here.
