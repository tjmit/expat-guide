Two years, then renewable for successive three-year periods.

## Citations and legal explanation

- Art. 75(1): temporary residence permits are valid two years and renewable in three-year periods, subject to special rules in other laws.
- The sentence on gov.pt about renewing "every five years" matches art. 76(2), which concerns permanent residence titles, not temporary ones.
