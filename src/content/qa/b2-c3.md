Yes, user fees still exist, with exemptions such as pregnant women, children up to 12, and people with low income.

## Citations and legal explanation

- Automatic exemptions include pregnant women, children up to 12, people with 60% or more disability, blood donors and transplant patients. Economic exemption applies if average monthly household income is not above 805.70 euros in 2026 (1.5 times the IAS of 537.13 euros), reassessed yearly by 30 September.
